# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want RunAware to be a respectful and productive open-source project.

Participants are expected to be professional, constructive, and considerate when interacting in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give actionable, technical feedback.
- Keep discussion focused on the project.
- Assume good intent when possible.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope and direction.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Publishing private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of project discussions
- Deliberately submitting malicious code or misleading reports

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants who violate this code of conduct.
